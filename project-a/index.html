[ Tim Tam ]

Scientific Name:

Timtamaserous

Discovered by / at:

Gavyn Uhl, Feb 2026

Family:

This creature belongs to the Timtamaserous family and was discovered on the floor of La Fin after a holliday weekend

Habitat:

The floor of La Fin on any weekend night.

Appearance:

A small black blob that glides around the ground with a distinctive wobbling motion, but dont touch it or it will get mad.

Key Characteristics:

  • Glides on the ground in the dark.
  • Eats dried floor alcohol.
  • Easily angered.

What we know so far about Tim Tam? Tim Tam was discovered in the early morning, 5:30am by our top scientist Gavyn Uhl. It at first reacted in an almsot friendly curious manner. However, when Gavyn tried to interact with it, it got very angry and started chasing him around the floor. It was only after Gavyn ran into the bathroom and locked the door that it stopped chasing him and return to a normal state. Scientist watched it for the next few days as it avoided party goers by gliding around and feeded on their spilt alchol.
Guidelines for human interaction with this creature It is still new to people looking at it so caution is advided. It will tolerate touching up until the 3rd tap where it will burst into a red, angry state and start chasing you around. If you find yourself in this situation, run to the nearest bathroom and lock the door. It will eventually calm down and return to its normal state.
